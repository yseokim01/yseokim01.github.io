Biography

Yeongseo Kim is an integrated master’s–Ph.D. student in the Department of Veterinary Medicine at Seoul National University. She is a member of the Veterinary Biochemistry Lab under the supervision of Professor Hyunjung Park. She received her B.S. in Food Science and Technology from Chungang University in 2025, graduating Summa Cum Laude. Her research interests include neuroimmune interactions, hematopoietic stem cells, biomechanics, aging, and the immune system.
